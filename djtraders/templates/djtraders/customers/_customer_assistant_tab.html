How the assistant answers
Ask something and each stage fills in here.
  1. Your question, as you typed it, or the button you pressed.
  2. The tools. With a language model set up, the model reads your question and asks for the tools it needs; without one, the assistant scores every tool against your words and picks one.
  3. Checks. Every tool must be in the registry for you and its arguments in range, whoever chose it. The customer comes from the login, never from the question or the model.
  4. The tools run. Each shows its method and where to read its code.
  5. The AI's text. When the model writes, you see what it was sent, what it wrote, and the check of every number and product name against what the tools returned.
